2 · How to play
  1. 🎯Find suspects. Red arrows at the edge of the screen point to them, and they show as red dots on the minimap (top right).
  2. 🟡Fill the gold ring to catch them. Stay close to a suspect to fill it slowly, ram them to stun them, or throw a Notice with Space to fill it fast.
  3. ✉️Notices are limited. Drive over the evidence suspects drop, or the ✉️ Notice stacks on the road, to get more.
  4. ⏳Every case has its own timer, shown under the suspect's name. If it hits zero, the case is time-barred and they get away.
  5. 🍗Extras: chicken rice gives a speed boost, catching suspects back to back earns a streak bonus, and ERP gantries cost $2. Yes, even us.
